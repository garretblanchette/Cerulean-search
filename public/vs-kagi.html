Cerulean vs Kagi

Kagi is a paid search engine (~$10/month). It is the most ambitious indie search product right now. Built by a real company with real engineering depth.

Kagi is better than Cerulean at

  • Index quality. They crawl their own plus aggregate.
  • Result ranking sophistication (real ML).
  • Lens features (programmable result filtering).
  • Long-term commitment (paid product with revenue).
  • Image search, maps, other modes.

Cerulean is better than Kagi at

  • Free. No account required.
  • Source-type classification visible by default.
  • AI-content filtering visible by default.
  • Faster (no auth round-trip; static deploy).

If you can afford $10/month and want a serious primary search engine, use Kagi. Cerulean is closer to a hobby project than a Kagi competitor.

The reason to use Cerulean instead: you do not want to pay, or you specifically want the source-classification UI.
