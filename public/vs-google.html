Cerulean vs Google

Cerulean is not trying to replace Google.

Google is better at

  • Universal coverage. Largest index.
  • Knowledge panels and instant answers.
  • Local results, maps, business listings.
  • Image search.
  • Multilingual coverage.

Cerulean does none of these well. It is a text-based web search.

Where Cerulean is better

  • No AI summaries pushing answers down or out of view.
  • No ads.
  • Source-type tags on every result.
  • AI-content filter on by default.

If you want Google with fewer commercial pressures, the closest equivalent is udm=14 mode: google.com/search?q=YOUR+QUERY&udm=14. That strips the AI overview, knowledge panels, and most ads. Cerulean adds source classification on a different (smaller) index.

Useful for: research, looking for human-written sources, avoiding AI slop.
Not useful for: anything Google's knowledge graph handles natively.
