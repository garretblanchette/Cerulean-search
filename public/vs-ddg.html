Cerulean vs DuckDuckGo

DuckDuckGo is the largest privacy-focused search engine. Free, claims not to track, good coverage via Bing as upstream.

DDG is better than Cerulean at

  • Index size (uses Bing's full index).
  • Privacy guarantees (formal policy, audited).
  • Instant answer integration.
  • Image search.
  • Mobile app, browser extensions, full product ecosystem.

Cerulean is better than DDG at

  • AI-content filtering as a default-on feature.
  • Source-type tags on every result (DDG just shows the domain).
  • Intent presets (Learn / Latest / Vibe Check / Shop / Primary Sources).
  • Filtering by source category.

DDG is the right default for privacy-conscious search. Cerulean is the right default for source-type filtering and AI-slop avoidance. Both fine to use together for different queries.
